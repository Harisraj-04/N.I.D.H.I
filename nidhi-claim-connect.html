📌 Problem Statement & Research Gap Identified

Why eligible insurance money fails to reach families after a policyholder's demise

1. Unknown Policies

Families and legal heirs are often completely unaware that the deceased held active or paid-up policies.

2. Scattered Records

Policy details are fragmented across multiple private and public life, health, and accident insurers.

3. Missing Information

Physical policy bonds or digital emails are difficult to trace during grief and emergencies.

4. Claim Confusion

Nominees do not know which insurer, branch, or repository to approach to initiate a claim.

5. Unclaimed Benefits

Eligible death and insurance benefits lie dormant, swelling ₹8,973.89 Cr+ in unclaimed funds.

The Core Problem & Research Gap: Existing systems like Bima Bharosa, DigiLocker, and insurance repositories operate separately. Families lose protection simply because they don't know it exists. N.I.D.H.I. is the automated connecting mechanism.

🚀 Innovation & Impact Comparison

Shifting from reactive manual searching to proactive automated beneficiary protection

# Existing Approach (Manual & Siloed) N.I.D.H.I. Innovation (Automated Digital Bridge)
1 Beneficiaries must search manually for policies without knowing what exists. Death event triggers automatic discovery of any linked insurance policies.
2 Individual insurer-by-insurer searches with repeated paperwork. Proposed cross-insurer discovery across participating insurers simultaneously.
3 Nominees have to locate and contact insurers on their own. Proactive nominee notification via automated SMS and Email alerts.
4 Separate, disconnected digital systems and silos. Integrated digital framework linking registry, repositories, and insurers.
5 Claims only begin long after beneficiary self-discovery (if ever). Claim handoff assistance begins immediately with automated policy discovery.
6 Bima Bharosa, DigiLocker, insurer DBs, and PIR (Proposed) only provide passive access. NEW Paradigm: A person's registered death actively triggers policy identification and alerts nominees & insurers.
